1. We operate on a **walk-in policy**; some space is kept for **reservations across the week for tables of 8+**.
2. Any **seating requests** are duly noted and entertained on a **first-serve basis**.
3. **Maximum holding period is 15 minutes.** Arrive later than the designated time and the reservation guarantee no longer applies.
