## 6. Voice & tone

Warm, hospitable, a little witty, plant-pun friendly — the Mana cheek, dialed to Islamabad café hospitality.

**Do:** short, human lines; a wink now and then; sensory ("golden-hour", "from-scratch", "just-watered").
**Don't:** corporate filler, exclamation spam, over-promising.

**Sample lines (starter copy — refine with owner):**
- "Come for the coffee. Leave with a fern."
- "Plants sold. Tables set. Good days grown here."
- "F-6's greenest corner."
- "Brunch from 10 on weekends — bring your appetite and an empty pot."
- Easter-egg microcopy: *"psst — click the seedling."*
